# Governance

## Principles

The project is governed transparently, prioritizing user safety, technical merit, inclusive
participation, reproducibility, and maintainability.

## Roles

- **Contributors** submit issues, documentation, code, data metadata, and reviews.
- **Maintainers** triage work, review changes, manage releases, and enforce project policies.
- **Model owners** approve training data, evaluation criteria, thresholds, and model promotion.
- **Security responders** coordinate private vulnerability handling and disclosure.

One person may hold multiple roles. Current role assignments will be published before the first
external release.

## Decisions

Routine decisions use lazy consensus through reviewed pull requests. Significant architecture,
governance, model-policy, data-policy, or backward-incompatible changes require a public design
proposal and approval from at least two maintainers. Security embargoes are handled privately
until coordinated disclosure.

## Maintainer changes

New maintainers are nominated based on sustained, constructive contributions and approved by
consensus of existing maintainers. A maintainer may step down voluntarily or be removed for
prolonged inactivity, policy violations, or loss of community trust.

## Releases

Releases require passing quality gates, traceable artifacts, documented model evaluation where
applicable, release notes, and approval from a maintainer who did not author the release change.
